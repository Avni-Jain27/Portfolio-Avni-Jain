Projects

Sanjay Lake Sustainability Study

Group project for Environmental Sciences, done with an NGO. Group leader.
Location: Sanjay Lake, Mayur Vihar / Trilokpuri, East Delhi

A study of the lake's sustainability, based on a one-day field visit and scored on an 8-parameter matrix: Energy, Water, Air, Soil, Biodiversity, Green infrastructure, Waste management, and Human interaction and awareness. Each parameter is scored from 1 to 5, normalised to a score out of 10, and combined into a composite index using equal weights. The final report covers the location, environmental significance, field observations, major problems, a sustainability report card, and recommendations.

Tools and methods
iNaturalist (flora and fauna records), visitor interviews, AQI check, water and soil sampling (TDS and chemical tests), Excel scoring matrix
